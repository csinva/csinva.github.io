Notes on ML, AI, and neuroscience#

Working notes on machine learning, statistics, AI, and neuroscience, kept since 2015 and organized into a book.

Each chapter is one of the notes from csinva.io: mostly compact bullet points, equations and figures, with a short overview at the top and interactive figures where moving a slider says more than a paragraph would. The foundations come first (computer science, math, statistics), then machine learning, AI and neuroscience, and finally research overviews that collect papers on open problems.
