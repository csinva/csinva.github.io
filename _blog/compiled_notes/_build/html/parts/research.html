Research overviews

Research overviews#

These chapters are reading lists rather than lecture notes. Each collects papers on one research topic, grouped by approach, usually with a short note on what each paper does and a link to it. Recent entries sit next to the classic papers they build on.

The first group is about modern models: LLMs (prompting, architectures, mechanistic interpretability, automated science), embeddings and retrieval, transfer learning and adversarial robustness, and uncertainty. Interpretability, the longest list after LLMs, covers transparent models, post-hoc explanations and how to evaluate them. The rest cover the statistics and theory side (generalization, complexity, deep learning theory, causal inference, disentanglement, the scattering transform) and applications in medicine and in protein and RNA structure, plus a short list of science results that are simply interesting.
